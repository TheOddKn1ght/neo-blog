## My productivity 


*Near zero*. Lately, I've fallen into a procrastination trap. I wait for the mythical *"perfect moment"* to focus on work, but it never arrives. Even though I have uninterrupted time throughout the day, the excuse of needing complete silence keeps circling in my head. (Right now, I'm writing this instead of working on my Django project for uni – classic procrastination!)

The struggle is real – tasks can feel uninspiring, and on the flip side, I haven't discovered any hobbies that truly excite me. During free time after exams, I fill the void with activities like endless Twitter scrolling or mindless gaming, but this "rest" feels hollow. It's a vicious cycle: I wake up with the intention to be productive, but the "later" monster whispers in my ear, and procrastination wins again.

I really want to break free from this cycle and find a way to use my free time productively and discover activities that bring me genuine joy.
